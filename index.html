Gameplay Programmer (Systems) · Ubisoft Québec Sep 2025 – Feb 2026
Confidential production · abstracted case study, no internal assets shown

Exploration systems & debug tooling on Anvil

Part of a cross-production Gameplay World Systems team, working in C++ on Ubisoft's proprietary Anvil engine — a large, shared codebase spanning five AAA productions.

I developed and maintained exploration gameplay systems and tools, and built an ImGui debug/visualization panel layered over an existing 2D map so teams could inspect, filter, and debug points of interest and world markers directly — cutting down investigation time during iteration. I also refactored a shared filtering module into a reusable component with a dedicated class and a cleaner API, removing duplication across tools.

Day to day meant code review through Swarm, coordinating with QA on reproduction and edge cases, and holding the line on coding guidelines and tech debt in a codebase other teams depend on.
